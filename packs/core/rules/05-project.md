**What it does.** One paragraph: the product, and who uses it.

**Domain terms.** The three to five nouns that mean something specific here, and
what they mean. An agent that guesses at these will name things wrong all the
way down.

**External systems.** Anything this talks to that does not live in this repo -
payment providers, internal APIs, queues - and which of them have a sandbox.

**Constraints that the code does not show.** Compliance rules, a migration in
progress, a dependency on its way out, a deliberate shortcut and its expiry.

Until this is filled in, agents work from the code alone and will guess at
intent.
